Take the books out of Wave Accounting, checked and complete.

A desktop app for accountants and bookkeepers. Load what Wave can give you, let Wave Exit merge and check it once, then make as many outputs as you need: an archive for the client file, a review workbook, report PDFs, and files for the new system.

Outputs

The universal package is always made: it's the archive, and every other output can be rebuilt from it.

Universal package always

Every record as documented CSV files, with receipts, a manifest and the tie-out report.

  • Ledger, chart, contacts, invoices, bills, payments
  • Receipts, linked to their transactions
  • Tie-out report and every review answer

PDF report pack

For the client file.

  • Trial balance, P&L and balance sheet by year
  • AR/AP aging and general ledger
  • No receipts or line items

Excel workbook

For review: every table on its own sheet, with filters.

  • A trial balance that recomputes from the journal
  • Aging sheets
  • No receipt files

General ledger CSV

One row per line with debit and credit, for any system that imports journals.

  • Full history, or opening balances plus later activity
  • No line items or payment links

Bank statement files

Bank and card history as OFX and CSV for QuickBooks Online, Xero and others.

  • History in the new system's banking screen
  • Wave's categories: categorise there or use bank rules

Coming planned

QuickBooks Online and Xero import files, direct load into QuickBooks Online and Xero with preview, resume and undo, and Beancount/hledger.

The tie-out checks

Run on every conversion, shown on the Review screen and written to a one-page report for the client file.

  1. Every entry balances
  2. Total debits equal total credits
  3. Every account balance agrees with Wave
  4. Invoices, payments and accounts receivable agree
  5. Bills add up to accounts payable
  6. Nothing lost in reading: rows in equal rows out
  7. No transactions dated after the export

Your clients' books stay on your computer

  • Wave Exit reads from Wave and writes only to the folders you choose. There is no Wave Exit server, no analytics and no upload.
  • Projects are stored encrypted, with the key in your computer's keychain.
  • It only reads from Wave. It never changes anything in Wave.
  • The app can only open the files you pick or drop, and can only contact Wave.

Questions

My client is on Wave's free plan and can't give me access. Can I still convert their books?

Yes. The client downloads the transactions export (and receipts) from Wave's Data Export page and sends you the files. Wave Exit works from those alone and shows you what is lost without a connection: unused accounts, invoice line items, products, sales taxes, due dates and the balance check against Wave.

Is it safe to give Wave Exit a Wave access key?

The key is kept in your computer's keychain, for that one project, and Wave Exit only reads with it. Create the key yourself in Wave's Developer Portal, never ask a client to email you theirs, and revoke it in Wave when you're done.

Which systems can I move the books to?

Today: anything that imports a general ledger CSV or bank statement files (QuickBooks Online, Xero and most others), plus the archive, Excel and PDF outputs. QuickBooks Online and Xero import files, and loading directly into them, are next.

What can't be brought out of Wave?

Recurring invoice schedules, bank reconciliation status, bank-feed connections, and payroll detail beyond Wave's payroll exports, which Wave offers only for Canadian businesses. No supported route gives these. Keep the PDF report pack for each closed year.

When can I download it?

Wave Exit is in development. Signed installers for macOS and Windows come once the remaining steps are finished. Follow the project on GitHub.
